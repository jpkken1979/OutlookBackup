## Vibe

Ultra-refined dark precision with subtle violet energy and meticulous product polish.

## Best For

- B2B dashboards
- internal tools
- high-focus productivity products

## Palette Notes

Use dark charcoal layers with controlled violet accents and quiet separators for depth.

## Typography Notes

Use compact, crisp typography with clear density management and strong UI labels.

## Layout Notes

Favor tight but ordered spacing, split panes, and interface density that stays legible.

## Component Notes

Use understated panels, low-contrast dividers, polished table/list patterns, and motion that rewards precision.

## Remote Excerpt

Project management. Ultra-minimal, precise, purple accent.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
