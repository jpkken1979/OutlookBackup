## Vibe

Monochrome precision with exact spacing, crisp typography, and infrastructure-grade confidence.

## Best For

- developer platforms
- infra landing pages
- docs-led SaaS

## Palette Notes

Use near-black, white, and neutral grays as the base; reserve color for tiny status moments, code states, or accent gradients.

## Typography Notes

Favor compact sans typography with strong hierarchy and low ornamentation.

## Layout Notes

Use strict grids, measured gutters, and sections that feel intentionally engineered.

## Component Notes

Components should feel sharp, compact, and exact; use thin borders, low-radius surfaces, and restrained shadows.

## Remote Excerpt

Frontend deployment. Black and white precision, Geist font.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
