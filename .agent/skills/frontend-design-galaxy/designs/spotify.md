## Vibe

Dark entertainment energy with vivid green accents, bold typography, and media-first momentum.

## Best For

- media products
- consumer dashboards
- youthful subscription experiences

## Palette Notes

Anchor the system in black and dark gray, then use bright green sparingly for primary action and recognition.

## Typography Notes

Use bold, expressive typography with strong marketing rhythm.

## Layout Notes

Favor large media surfaces, stacked content modules, and fast-scanning shelves.

## Component Notes

Cards, nav rails, and featured content modules should feel immersive and energetic.

## Remote Excerpt

Music streaming. Vibrant green on dark, bold type, album-art-driven.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
