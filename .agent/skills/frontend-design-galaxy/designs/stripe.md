## Vibe

Elegant financial confidence powered by aurora gradients, soft surfaces, and premium detail.

## Best For

- fintech marketing
- API platforms
- trust-sensitive onboarding

## Palette Notes

Blend soft purples, blues, and neutrals with gradient lighting used as atmosphere rather than decoration.

## Typography Notes

Use light-to-regular weight display typography balanced with very readable body text.

## Layout Notes

Prefer layered sections, floating cards, and clean demo spaces with premium margins.

## Component Notes

Cards, pricing blocks, and trust banners should feel luminous, polished, and enterprise-ready.

## Remote Excerpt

Payment infrastructure. Signature purple gradients, weight-300 elegance.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
