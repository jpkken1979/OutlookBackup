## Vibe

Motion-forward black-and-blue design language with bold contrast and creative confidence.

## Best For

- creative tool landing pages
- product launches
- motion-rich marketing

## Palette Notes

Use black, white, and electric blue accents with gradients reserved for hero surfaces and motion cues.

## Typography Notes

Use assertive display typography with modern, high-contrast body copy.

## Layout Notes

Favor dramatic hero sections, asymmetry, and high-contrast blocks that showcase motion.

## Component Notes

CTAs, feature cards, and media frames should feel kinetic, polished, and high impact.

## Remote Excerpt

Website builder. Bold black and blue, motion-first, design-forward.
