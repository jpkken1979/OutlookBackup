## Vibe

Warm editorial clarity with terracotta accents, calm whitespace, and thoughtful reading rhythm.

## Best For

- AI chat interfaces
- knowledge products
- editorial control panels

## Palette Notes

Use warm neutrals, parchment-like surfaces, dark ink text, and a restrained terracotta accent for primary actions and highlights.

## Typography Notes

Favor highly readable text with literary contrast between display headlines and comfortable body copy.

## Layout Notes

Prefer generous breathing room, asymmetric editorial sections, and clean reading columns.

## Component Notes

Buttons and cards should feel calm and trustworthy; emphasize conversation surfaces, side panels, and annotation-friendly containers.

## Remote Excerpt

Anthropic's AI assistant. Warm terracotta accent, clean editorial layout.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
