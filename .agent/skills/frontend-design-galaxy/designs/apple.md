## Vibe

Premium restraint with luminous whitespace, cinematic product framing, and immaculate hierarchy.

## Best For

- premium product pages
- hardware or consumer launches
- brand-first experiences

## Palette Notes

Use whites, cool grays, and restrained blacks with color mostly delegated to media or product imagery.

## Typography Notes

Use large, confident headlines with meticulous spacing and minimal visual noise.

## Layout Notes

Use wide margins, generous section spacing, and media-led storytelling.

## Component Notes

Controls and cards should feel extremely refined, minimal, and physically plausible.

## Remote Excerpt

Consumer electronics. Premium white space, SF Pro, cinematic imagery.

## Integration Hints

- Start from semantic tokens, not raw copied colors.
- Keep the reference as a direction; adapt it to the product domain.
- Preserve accessibility and interaction clarity over aesthetic mimicry.
