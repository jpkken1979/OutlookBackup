# X Article Publisher

Publish long-form content to X (formerly Twitter) as optimized threads with proper formatting, engagement hooks, and character-aware splitting.

## Publishing Workflow

### 1. Content Preparation
- Break long-form articles into thread-appropriate chunks (280 char limit per X post)
- Identify natural breaking points and subtopic divisions
- Flag engagement opportunities (questions, surprising facts, patterns)

### 2. Thread Structure
- **Opening Hook**: Compelling first post that captures attention (questions, surprising stat, bold claim)
- **Body Posts**: 3-8 posts developing core ideas with clear progression
- **Closing**: Call-to-action, summarization, or invitation to discussion
- **Threading**: Use quote-retweet threading or numbered posts (1/8, 2/8, etc.)

### 3. Content Optimization
- Remove filler words and redundancy
- Use line breaks and spacing for readability
- Add hashtags strategically (2-3 per thread, less per post)
- Include links to full article or relevant resources
- Optimize for searchability with trending keywords when relevant

### 4. Formatting for X
- **Character counting**: Each post must fit 280 character limit
- **Link handling**: Links consume 23 characters regardless of length
- **Emoji usage**: ~2 characters per emoji, use sparingly for emphasis
- **Hashtags**: Place at end when possible to preserve post flow
- **Mentions**: Tag relevant authors, sources, and audiences

## Publication Checklist

- [ ] Content is thread-worthy (5+ distinct ideas minimum)
- [ ] First post has strong hook (question, stat, or bold claim)
- [ ] Each post stands alone but connects to thread narrative
- [ ] Character counts verified for all posts
- [ ] Links formatted correctly (no display clutter)
- [ ] Hashtags chosen for discoverability without spam
- [ ] Call-to-action clear in final post
- [ ] Tone matches target audience and X culture

## Common Patterns

| Content Type | Thread Length | Hook Type |
|--------------|---------------|-----------|
| Tutorial/How-To | 5-8 posts | "Here's how to..." or step-by-step numbered |
| Research Summary | 6-10 posts | Surprising finding or contrarian take |
| Opinion/Take | 3-5 posts | Bold claim or question |
| Case Study | 7-12 posts | Outcome/result upfront, then explanation |
| Thread-Native | 4-6 posts | Story, process, or progression |
